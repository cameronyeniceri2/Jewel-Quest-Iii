## 📖 Overview
<a id="overview"></a>
Jewel Quest III is a captivating puzzle game that invites players to connect gems of the same color to make them disappear. This is the full version of the game, featuring all levels and updates included for an enriching gaming experience.

As part of an exciting series of puzzle video games, Jewel Quest III takes players on an adventure through a museum filled with challenges. The storyline follows archeologists Emma and Rupert, whose daughter Natalie becomes infected by magical spores while playing on a jewel board. Players must navigate through 11 unique regions in Central and South America to find a cure for Natalie by matching and clearing gems.
